# Requirements & Planning

Turn natural language into an implementation-ready specification.

Capture:
- business goal and primary conversion;
- personas and jobs-to-be-done;
- sitemap and navigation;
- functional requirements;
- content requirements;
- integrations;
- non-functional requirements;
- analytics events;
- acceptance criteria.

Use a priority matrix: must / should / could / deferred.

For ambiguous requirements, choose the safest reversible assumption and document it.
